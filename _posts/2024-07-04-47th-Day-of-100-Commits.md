# True, but depressing 

Still working through lessons, but I think I'm ready to work on projects again. Partially I'm avoiding them because with grief and getting over a cold my energy is low, and partially because I am discouraged by that gap between what I want to produce and what I'm capable of. I know there's only one way to bridge that gap, but I'm afraid of the frustration I will inevitably encounter on that way... My confidence/self-worth is pretty low, too. This stuff about my dad isn't helping. I wonder if he knew how much his lack of presence has contributed to my self-image, and struggle to trust others. I wish I could say there's some superpower I've gained from his abandonment, but I mostly feel, and know I come across to others, as damaged, broken. Adding to that my struggle against the limiting belief that I'm too late to this game makes it clear it would be much easier, and make more sense, to quit spending so much of my precious time on this. I think if I were still drinking, I would have quit already. I admit I'm persevering in part because of desperation for something to improve/make progress on _something_ in my life. Frankly, I don't have much else better to do... I should feel a bit better when I get back to my exercise routine, and when I get in the groove at my new job.
